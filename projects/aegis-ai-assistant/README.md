# AEGIS — Voice-First AI Assistant / GOKU Humanoid Vision

**Status: Product concept and engineering roadmap. Not a working humanoid or fully deployed assistant.**

AEGIS is a proposed modular Windows-based assistant with voice interaction, contextual memory, research, configurable permissions, and human-approved action workflows for Triod Health Care.

## Proposed architecture
1. Voice interface: speech-to-text, text-to-speech, turn-taking and interruption handling
2. Context service: consent-based memory, personalization and source-grounded answers
3. Tools: research, task preparation, business outreach drafts and approval gates
4. Future GOKU track: computer vision, physical robot safety, sensors and supervised control

## Current stage
Planning and design documentation, with future milestones for executable local prototypes and hardware experiments. This repository does not claim working robotics hardware, general autonomy or production integrations.
